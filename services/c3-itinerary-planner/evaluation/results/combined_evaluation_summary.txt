KEY FINDINGS
Optimizer results show region-dependent coverage/travel trade-offs rather than uniform distance reduction.
Medical-access-aware weighting changes route selection and increases travel in this controlled scenario.
Route filtering substantially narrows the candidate space; detour candidates generally have low modeled insertion times.
Production uses ABSA-aware ranking when review evidence is available and fallback ranking when it is unavailable.
Banana Chill ordering differs from detour-only ordering; ABSA alone cannot be credited for the rank shifts.

LIMITATIONS
The three-region evaluation is a prototype-stage sample, not full nationwide validation.
The medical experiment is one controlled Kandy scenario. Hospital access values are modeled road travel times and do not guarantee emergency response.
Restaurant ABSA coverage is limited: one unique Kandy restaurant on this route had ABSA evidence, appearing on three days.
Ranking effects cannot isolate ABSA from cuisine preference. Detour-only ties share minimum rank; production ranks are day-specific.
Missing location sentiment remains missing; no sentiment evidence was invented.
The earlier historical OR-Tools 14-POI control discrepancy remains unexplained; current repeated runs were stable.
Greedy driving times retain fractional minutes; optimizer driving times use production rounded-per-leg minutes, so small differences need cautious interpretation.
Filtering counts use unique restaurants; detour/ranking counts use restaurant/day rows. Corridor reduction also includes production coordinate cleaning.
Regional figures use the user's approved valid results embedded in this reporting script; medical and restaurant figures come from the existing source CSVs. No evaluations were rerun.
